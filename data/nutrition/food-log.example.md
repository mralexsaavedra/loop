# Food log

> TEMPLATE. Copy to `data/nutrition/food-log.md` and start logging (that filename is
> gitignored — personal data, stays local).
>
> Apple Health does NOT store what you eat unless an app writes it. To judge whether
> intake is good or bad, this file needs to exist. Keep it simple — consistency beats
> precision. Minimum useful entry: date + what you ate per meal. Add quantities when you
> can; rough portions are enough for Claude to estimate trends.

## Format

```
## YYYY-MM-DD
- Breakfast: <food, rough amount>
- Lunch: <food, rough amount>
- Dinner: <food, rough amount>
- Snacks: <...>
- Drinks (esp. alcohol/sugary): <...>
- Notes: <hunger, energy, anything off>
```
